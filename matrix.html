Bicolano; Bicol People
Ilocano; Ilocano People

Hero Profile

Three heroes across epochs: Baltog (conqueror), Handyong (civilizer), Bantong (liberator)
Single supernatural hero Lam-ang with extraordinary birth, strength, and companions

Central Conflict

Civilization vs. chaos; heroes subdue mythical monsters (Oryol, Tandayag, Rabot) to build society
Personal vengeance and love quest; individual hero defying death for honor and family

Supernatural Elements

Monster creatures (serpents, giant boars, multi-headed animals), natural catastrophes, shapeshifters
Miraculous birth, speaking animals (rooster & dog), resurrection from the dead, berkakan sea creature

Core Cultural Values

Communal resilience, environmental stewardship, rule of law, collective civilization-building
Filial piety, individual heroism, romantic love, loyalty between human and animal companions

Transmission & Preservation

Oral tradition; recorded by Franciscan friars; rediscovered by Dr. Merito Espinas from a 19th-century manuscript
First transcribed ~1640 by Pedro Bucaneg; published in 1889 by Isabelo de los Reyes; compiled by Leopoldo Yabes
