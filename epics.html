Philippine Folk Epics

Explore the foundational narratives of regional oral literature. Our archive profiles the major epic narratives representing the Bicolano and Ilocano cultural heritage.

Bicolano Oral Epic

IBALONG

A legendary fragment recounting the mythological origin of the Bicol region. The story revolves around three successive warrior-heroes—Baltog, Handyong, and Bantong—who fought mythical beasts, established the first laws, and cultivated civilization in the ancient lands of Ibalon.

Ilocano Folk Legend

BIAG NI LAM-ANG

Reciting the magical life adventures of the hero Lam-ang of Nalbuan. Born with supernatural speech and physical strength, he sets forth to avenge his father's death against enemies, wins the hand of the beautiful Ines Cannoyan with his magical companions, and triumphs over mortality itself.
